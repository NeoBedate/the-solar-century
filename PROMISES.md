# Promises

Things we have said we will build or decide later.

**Game flow**
- Load Game and Options on the title screen.
- Easy and Hard difficulties (only Normal is available).
- The original-style main screen images. Every location has a CSS placeholder scene today (the Moonbase one included); they are to be replaced by real art.

**Design to define**
- Food and consumption balance: food, the Hydroponics building (needs water, nitrogen and power) and the per-lifeform rates exist, but all numbers are provisional. The starting Moonbase (80 colonists, 2 habitats of 50, 1 solar array, 1 mine, 2 ice extractors, 1 Hydroponics; it begins 34% short of power on purpose, as the first tutorial need) was adjusted so that it survives. Staffing was multiplied by 4 and building outputs (resources and power, not inputs or research) by 2; construction sites still need 2 builders, and the Habitat module cost is 4 times the old one. Still to define: food on colony ships and colony supplies, other food sources, different diets per lifeform, what the Water recycling technology (the old Hydroponics technology, renamed) should become, and whether Hydroponics should need its own technology.
- Technologies for the Manufacturing and Weapons research categories. The filters exist, but no technology belongs to them yet.
- Balancing resources and costs, which is a job for later down the road. All values are provisional.
- A source for software.
- Other ways to earn research points besides the Science Lab, such as performing other tasks.
- Where to display the number of colonised locations. It is tracked already, but hidden for now.

**Expansion**
- Drive technologies: specify several drives (faster than the simplest one, which covers 1 AU in 1946 days) so that, over a whole game, every location can be reached. The simplest drive cannot reach beyond Uranus before 2200. Probe trip times must then use the drive's speed instead of the single `SURVEY.dpa`.
- Colonisation, next details from the owner: what a finished colonisation module does (launching it and founding the colony), what the probe analysis reveals about each location, and the real values for each location's analysis difficulty, length, trip time and technology cost. Everything is placeholder today, and a failed analysis only costs time.
- Atmosphere: give each location an atmosphere value based on its controlling atmospheric resource. It decides which environmental technology is needed to sustain a colony there.
- Environmental technology: define the technologies that let a colony survive each atmosphere type (to be tied to the location's atmosphere value).
- Starship parts: every starship gets these parts, each tied to a technology: drive, engine, life support, cargo bay, crew quarters, bridge, ship's computer, laboratory, weapons and shields.
- Minimum crew warning: every starship needs a minimum crew to work properly. When the crew aboard falls below that minimum, issue a warning (define the minimum per ship type and how the warning is shown).
- Starship loading, next steps: the Crew, Cargo and Resources tabs of each starship exist (Cargo is empty). Crew quarters should limit how many people can be aboard, the Cargo tab needs its content (parts and colony modules in the cargo bay), and missions do not yet use the crew and resources loaded on a ship.
- Refit: the button exists on each starship but is disabled. Define how a starship's parts are changed (cost, time, which parts, whether it must be landed).
- Drives, still to wire: the five drives exist as data (Solar Sail, Hydrolox, Ion, Nuclear, Sublight: speed in days per AU, fuel per AU, weight, build cost, technology; editable under Starships in the Editor, tabulated in the GDD) but only the Solar Sail is used, by every probe. Still to do: build each drive, choose or fit a drive per ship (Refit), trip time from the drive's speed, fuel burnt per AU replacing the fixed hydrogen and oxygen cost of missions (the owner chose replace, and fuel weight counts toward tonnage), Ion fuel is sulphur, uranium supply for Nuclear and Sublight, and the Outer-system drive technology, which now overlaps the Nuclear Drive.
- Events: the mechanic exists (Events table, editable in the Editor, listed in the GDD) with one type, accident, and one event: a 3% chance per flight of a Sublight Drive accident that destroys the ship and its crew (unused until ships can carry that drive). Still to do: more event types and events (for other drives, space hazards, the base), effects other than losing the ship (damage, delays, repairs), a warning before launch, and moving the existing random monthly events of the Moonbase (meteor, vein, solar flare) into the same Events table.
- Engines, still to wire: the four engines exist as data (Tokamak Fusion Reactor v1.0, v2.0 and v3.0, and the Super Tokamak Fusion Reactor: power yield, fuel per month, weight, build cost, technology; editable in the Editor, tabulated in the GDD) but no ship carries one and ship power is not used yet. To decide: what the ship's power runs (drive, lab, shields, weapons), what happens when it falls short, and building and fitting engines (Refit). All four burn hydrogen.
- Life support, still to wire: the four systems exist as data (Open-loop tanks, Closed-loop bioregenerative system, Hydroponic ring, Biosphere: renewal rate of the oxygen consumed, energy consumed, weight, build cost, technology; editable in the Editor, tabulated in the GDD) but no ship carries one, and ship oxygen and power are not used yet. To decide: whether the energy is fixed per system (assumed) or grows with the people aboard, the oxygen a ship carries and burns per person, what happens when oxygen or power runs out, and building and fitting systems (Refit).
- Define the starship parts: what each one does and its values, per starship type (to be defined later).
- Cargo and weight: weights exist (per unit of each resource, per part type and per crew member, all provisional, editable under Starships in the Editor) but nothing uses them yet. The Starships overview shows each ship's total tonnage (fitted parts plus crew) over a maximum (placeholder: 100 t for probes). Still to define: how the maximum is set (by the drive, the cargo bay or the ship type), cargo bay capacity, what loading a starship means, and how total weight affects the drive (speed, fuel or trip time). Weights per part type will need to become per part variant once there are several drives and so on.
- Colony module, still to define: what it is physically, its size and contents. Its cost exists (titanium 1100, aluminium 700, copper 100, silica 400, platinum 100, components 200, machinery 100 (my proportions, to review), plus a year of its lifeform's gas, water and food for 50 colonists, which the owner confirmed as the module's size, and a "Colony module" technology per lifeform, all provisional). To colonise, a starship must carry the module in its cargo bay (Cargo tab still empty).
- Colonists travel on the same starship as the colony module and occupy its crew quarters.
- Colonist lifeforms: colonists are Human, Martian, Hydrogoid, Nitroid, Sulphuroid or Methanoid (the owner's table names Titan's lifeform Nitroid; confirm), each based on the atmospheric conditions of a location. All have "Colonist" as base job and any can work as Labourer, Scientist, Technician, Builder or Crew. Locations now carry a lifeform and the table's resources (see Database). Still to define: Leda, Hyperion and Phoebe are now in the game with placeholder scenes (real art still to come), Mercury, Earth and the asteroid belt have no lifeform (Mercury is uncolonisable; Earth for narrative reasons, the belt for its composition), the resources of Venus, Io, Jupiter, Europa, Ganymede, Dione, Phobos, Deimos, Iapetus, Mimas and Charon are my extrapolation from the table and need the owner's review, how a lifeform ties to the atmosphere value and environmental technology, any differences between lifeforms (needs, jobs, births), and how the code changes (today jobs and the ship crew kinds treat Colonists, Labourers, etc. as separate kinds).
- Allegiance: every colonist has a secret variable, allegiance, that puts them on the player's side. To define later: what it implies, how it goes up and down, and what that means for the game. It stays hidden from the player.
- Factions: the game will have at least one other faction from the beginning, the Martians. To define later: how many factions there are, how they relate to the lifeforms and to the player, where they live, and what they do.
- Colony supplies: the module now includes a year of gas, water and food for 50 colonists; whether the ship must carry more is still to define.
- Map: starships and missions shown flying on the map and in orbit, and real art for the orbit globes. The orbit view only shows the globe with a marker. Moons are drawn on circular orbits with arbitrary starting positions (their real eccentricities and positions are not used), and the spacing is compressed (exponent per system in `SYSG`; the far irregular moons Leda and Phoebe sit on a dashed outer ring, listed in `IRR`).
- Founding colonies on other planets, moons and in the asteroid belt. They are listed in Colonies but only the Moonbase exists.
- Several outposts running different research projects at the same time. The Under Investigation tab of the Mission Control Log is built for this.
- The other starship types: Hop Miners, Solar Carriers, Nuclear Cruisers and Colony Ships. They can be researched and built, and today only Probes exist.
