# The Solar Century

A single-file browser prototype of a lunar-colony resource management game, inspired by *Millennium 2.2* (1989). Earth is lost; build up a Moon base and launch the Gaia Ark before the year 2300.

**Status:** early prototype. All costs, rates and timings are provisional.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step and no dependencies. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online; the game falls back to system monospace fonts otherwise.

## What is in the prototype

- Title screen with New Game, Load Game, Options and Exit. New Game offers a difficulty screen (only Normal is available).
- Hour-by-hour clock with five speeds, from 1 hour per 30 seconds to 1 hour per half second.
- Moonbase main screen linking to Construction, Science Labs, Shipyard, Flight plans and the Mission Control Log.
- 18 resources in five categories (Volatiles, Minerals, Metals, Rare metals, Manufactured).
- Buildings with construction time, power use and machinery upkeep.
- Research projects: passive research points, upfront and daily costs, pause and resume.
- Missions to Earth, Mars, the asteroid belt and the outer moons.
